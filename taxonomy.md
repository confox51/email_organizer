# Email Taxonomy

This document defines the mutually exclusive categories used for organizing emails in this project. These categories were derived from an analysis of ~200 real emails and refined based on user feedback.

## 1. Travel & Logistics
*   **Description**: Reservations, flight confirmations, refunds, and trip details.
*   **Examples**: Airbnb, Chase Travel, Frontier Airlines, AwardWallet, IHG.

## 2. Career & Professional
*   **Description**: Job applications, interviews, recruiter correspondence, and professional networking.
*   **Examples**: Arnata, Backflip, Nova Talent, Earnd, SpotOnTalent, Resume-related threads.

## 3. Financial & Investments
*   **Description**: Bank statements, credit card notices, tax documents, and dedicated **Investment** tracking (brokerage accounts, portfolio updates).
*   **Examples**: Chase, Raymond James, Inspira Financial (Tax), EDD.

## 4. Health & Insurance
*   **Description**: Communication regarding medical appointments, **therapy sessions**, health insurance claims, COBRA, and other insurance policies.
*   **Examples**: MyUCLAhealth, McMaster (Benefits/Insurance), Inspira (HSA), therapy appointments or invoices.

## 5. Material Purchases & Gifts
*   **Description**: Specific receipts for physical goods, material objects, and gifts purchased online or in-store.
*   **Examples**: Sunglass Fix, e-commerce retailers, gift notifications.

## 6. Education & Academic
*   **Description**: Communication from MIT, Sloan School, and related academic/student organizations.
*   **Examples**: mit.edu, sloan.mit.edu.

## 7. Daily Services & Utilities
*   **Description**: Food delivery, entertainment bookings, service subscriptions (non-material), and general notifications.
*   **Examples**: DoorDash, Eventbrite.

## 8. Housing & Real Estate
*   **Description**: Rent payments, lease agreements, maintenance requests, mortgage documents, property insurance, and real estate listings. **Includes** finding roommates (even with personal details), paying rent/fees (even if financial), and correspondence with agents/landlords (even if professional).
*   **Examples**: Round Room Boston, Weichert, Zillow, Apartments.com.

## 9. Library
*   **Description**: Library holds, due date reminders, digital loans, and library events.
*   **Examples**: Overdrive, Libby, Alexandria Library, Indianapolis Public Library.

## 10. Community & Local
*   **Description**: Local government notifications, school board updates, neighborhood associations, and community newsletters.
*   **Examples**: Enroll Indy, Arlington County, Nextdoor.

## 11. Personal & One-Offs
*   **Description**: Direct correspondence from individuals that don't fall into the categories above.
*   **Examples**: Personal Gmail contacts.
